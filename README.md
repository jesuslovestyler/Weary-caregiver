# For the Weary Caregiver

A tender page of Scripture for weary caregivers — strength and rest for the ones who hold everyone else up: caring for aging parents, sick spouses, special-needs children, anyone who pours out daily.

Sections: God sees your labor, strength for today, rest for your soul, compassion that mirrors His, and you are not alone in this. All verses from the World English Bible (public domain).
